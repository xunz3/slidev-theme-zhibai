# Structure & meaning · 结构与意义

<span data-font-prose="latin">Good evidence makes its assumptions visible. State the question, explain the method, and give the result enough context to be understood.</span>

<span data-font-prose="cjk" lang="zh-CN">表达需要清晰的层次，也需要足够的信息。让标题、正文、公式与图注各有位置，而不是依赖过大的留白。</span>

<Callout type="note" title="A boundary worth recording">

The type hierarchy should clarify the relationship between the claim and its supporting evidence.

</Callout>
