# Let the evidence speak.

Good slides give an idea enough space to be understood.

<v-clicks>

- **Hierarchy** — one clear entry point, followed by supporting detail.
- **Rhythm** — consistent alignment from the first slide to the last.
- **Restraint** — color and movement with a specific purpose.

</v-clicks>

<Callout type="note" title="A note in the margin · 旁注">
Keep the argument visible; let the supporting details stay quiet.
</Callout>
